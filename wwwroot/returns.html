Returns & Refunds

We want StrideX purchases to arrive in the condition and specification you expected. This policy explains how standard-product returns, refunds, exchanges and Custom Kit issues are handled.

Standard products

Change-of-mind returns may be requested within 30 calendar days after delivery or collection, provided the product is unused, unworn and returned with its original tags and packaging where reasonably possible.

Proof of purchase

Keep your StrideX receipt or order number. You can find both from My Orders. We use the order record to confirm the item, purchase date and amount paid.

Damaged, incorrect or defective items

If an item arrives damaged, is incorrect, or appears defective, contact StrideX as soon as reasonably possible. We will review the order and arrange an appropriate remedy where applicable.

Custom Teamwear

Because Custom Kits are made to the team specifications submitted with the request, change-of-mind returns are generally not available once production has started. This does not apply where the supplied kit is defective or does not match the confirmed order.

Refunds

Once an approved return has been received and inspected, the refund is processed back to the original payment method. Bank processing times can vary after the refund has been issued.

Exchanges

Exchanges depend on current stock availability. If the requested replacement size or product is unavailable, a refund or another suitable resolution can be arranged.

How to request a return

  1. Open My Orders and locate the relevant order.
  2. Open View Details and note the order number and item.
  3. Email StrideX with the order number, item, reason for return and any useful photos.
  4. Wait for return instructions before sending the product back.
